# MediCare – Hospital Management System

## Project Overview

MediCare is a Hospital Management System designed to make patient record management and appointment booking easier. It aims to reduce the problems caused by maintaining patient information and appointments manually.

## Problem Statement

Small clinics and hospitals often use paper registers to maintain patient records, doctor schedules, and appointments. This can lead to misplaced files, appointment conflicts, and delays in finding patient information.

## Objectives

* Maintain patient records digitally.
* Display doctors and their specializations.
* Check doctor availability.
* Book and manage appointments.
* Maintain medical history and prescriptions.
